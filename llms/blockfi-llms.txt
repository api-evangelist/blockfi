# BlockFi

> BlockFi was a Jersey City, New Jersey crypto-financial-services company (founded 2017
> by Zac Prince and Flori Marquez) that offered interest-bearing crypto accounts,
> crypto-collateralized USD loans, spot trading, and a bitcoin-rewards credit card. It
> filed for Chapter 11 in November 2022 after the FTX/Alameda collapse and emerged in
> October 2023 as a wind-down estate. **BlockFi is not an operating company and has no
> API.** blockfi.com now serves only bankruptcy-estate notices.

Generated by API Evangelist (https://apievangelist.com) on 2026-08-07.
Method: generated from apis.yml plus live probes. BlockFi publishes no llms.txt of its
own — https://blockfi.com/llms.txt returns HTTP 404.

## Status

- Operating: **no**. Chapter 11 filed 2022-11-28; plan effective October 2023.
- Customer web platform and mobile apps: shut down.
- In-kind crypto distributions: administered through Coinbase.
- Cash distributions: administered through Kroll Restructuring Administration and
  Digital Disbursements.
- Latest estate notice published on blockfi.com: 2025-04-16.

## API surface

**None.** As of 2026-08-07 there is no machine-readable contract of any kind:

- No OpenAPI or Swagger document. `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`,
  `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/redoc` and `/docs` all return 404 on blockfi.com.
- No GraphQL endpoint. `/graphql` returns 404.
- No MCP server. `/mcp` and `/.well-known/mcp.json` return 404.
- No A2A agent card. `/.well-known/agent-card.json` and the legacy
  `/.well-known/agent.json` both return 404.
- No AsyncAPI, event stream, or webhook catalog.
- No `/.well-known/` document of any kind, including no security.txt.
- No client SDK in npm, PyPI, RubyGems, crates.io, Packagist, NuGet or Maven Central.
- The GitHub organization https://github.com/blockfi exists but has **zero** public
  repositories.
- Every developer-facing host has been removed from DNS: api.blockfi.com,
  docs.blockfi.com, developer.blockfi.com, developers.blockfi.com, app.blockfi.com,
  portal.blockfi.com, status.blockfi.com, trade.blockfi.com.

## Notes for agents

- blockfi.com serves `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">` and its
  robots.txt names and disallows a long list of AI crawlers (Amazonbot, anthropic-ai,
  Applebot-Extended, Bytespider, CCBot, ClaudeBot and others). Honor it.
- The estate publishes repeated phishing warnings. Any site, form, wallet address or
  "recovery service" claiming to process BlockFi distributions and not linked from
  blockfi.com itself or from Kroll should be treated as hostile.
- Do not attempt to integrate with BlockFi. There is nothing to integrate with.
